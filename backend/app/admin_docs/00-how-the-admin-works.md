## What it is for

The admin is where you run your company with your agents: Nia, the agents you create, what they propose, your mail and marketing, and the health of the install. Everything a business value depends on (budgets, limits, schedules, texts, switches) is set here, not in the code.

## Two roles

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Everything: change settings, clear agents' proposals, budgets, invite and remove admins. |
| **Support** | Read and work in the admin: talk to Nia and the agents, read mail, look at Insights. Settings, admins and money stay greyed out. |

Where a page says "owner only", support admins see it but cannot change it. See [Admins](docs:admins).

## Every change is recorded

Each sign-in and each change is written to the [Admin log](docs:admin-log) with who, when, from which address, and the details. Nobody can edit or delete it.

## The sidebar

- **The bell** shows what needs your attention: alerts, open incidents and proposals that could not be carried out. Opening it marks them seen.
- **The "?"** opens the documentation for the page you are on. **Docs** at the bottom of the sidebar has all of it, with search.
- **Nia** (the round button at the bottom right) is your chief of staff: ask her what is going on, and she can show you around pages. See [Nia](docs:nia).

## Agents propose, you give clearance

The agents in [Team](docs:team) do the work, but anything with consequences outside the admin (sending, posting, spending, changing what customers see) waits in [Clearance](docs:approvals) until you clear it. How much an agent may do alone is set per agent, and money, deleting, settings and agents' own instructions are always yours.
