## What it is for

The HQ shows the team the way you would see a real headquarters. It is for showing the company to people (and for the fun of watching the team work); it only reads and changes nothing.

**The building**: dressed stone with tall arched windows, a columned portico at the entrance, marble halls, oak floors, steel beams and pendant lights. Walls that stand between you and a room turn to glass, so you always see in.

| Place | Who |
|---|---|
| **Founder's suite** (closed, north-west) | You: walnut desk, seating corner, books and art |
| **Nia's office** (closed, next to yours) | Nia, at her holographic table |
| **Boardroom** (glass) | Meetings |
| **West, centre, studio and east floors** (open) | Your agents: each new agent takes the next free desk, wearing its department's colour |
| **Quiet room** (glass back office) | The next agent once the floors are full |
| **Outside agents' wing** | Outside agents, at the desk you give them |
| Library, reception, café | — |

## What you see

| In the office | Means |
|---|---|
| Agent typing, screen scrolling, the green light at the desk's edge, a teal line above the head | The agent is running now; the line says what it is doing ("Searching the web", "Writing a proposal for you"). |
| Agent at the desk (looking around), blue light | Ready, between runs. Click it for the first line of its last report. |
| Agent with chin on hand, a "?" and an amber light | Waiting for a setting (shown in its card) — set it in [Team](docs:team). |
| Empty chair, dark screen, grey light | Switched off (or its stage is not built yet). |
| Agent standing at a colleague's desk with a speech bubble | It just handed work to that colleague (a handoff, for example "QA check failed"); it walks over and stays a few minutes, then goes back. |
| ★ You (gold ring) | Where you are: at your desk in the Founder's suite, or — while you talk with an agent (its chat in Team, or Nia) — standing at that agent's desk. One minute after the conversation goes quiet (no message from you or the agent) you walk back to your suite. |

The office follows your local time: daylight, evening, and the city lights at night.

## Working with it

- Drag to turn, scroll to zoom, right-drag to move. Click an agent or its office for its card: what it does, runs, proposals and AI cost today, what waits for you, **Talk to …** (its chat) and **Clearance**.
- The list on the left (wide screens) jumps to any agent; **Tour** flies from one busy office to the next by itself; the eye hides the names; the arrow shows the whole floor; full screen for presenting.
- The bar at the bottom lists the latest handoffs; click one to fly to it.
- The page refreshes every few seconds.
