## What it is for

A watch over two things that must not slip: the runs that delete data on time, and proposals that wait for your clearance longer than you allow. Agents with the right tools read the same and recommend; you decide.

## Data deletion runs

The runs that delete old data on time (finished tasks, e-mail records, old insight numbers). A late or failing run is shown in red with its error.

## Rules

| Rule | What it does | Empty |
|---|---|---|
| **Call out a proposal waiting for your clearance after (days)** | shown at the top and readable by agents | nothing called out |
